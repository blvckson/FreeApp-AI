    Phone Control (Android)

    FreeApp AI can open supported apps and perform visible screen actions after you enable its accessibility service. Enable it yourself in Android settings and keep the phone unlocked while actions run.

    For screen actions, enter one action per line. Supported: open Gmail/Settings/Chrome/Play Store/Files; tap: visible label; type: ordinary text into the currently focused field; scroll up/down; back; home. Actions are sent to the current screen, so watch the phone. FreeApp AI will not type passwords, one-time codes, security codes or card details. You must sign in yourself and approve sending emails, payments, deleting data, security changes, or other consequential actions. System updates still require Android's own confirmation.
